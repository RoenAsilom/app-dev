: **Initial D**🚗 is a popular Japanese manga and anime series about the world of illegal street racing in Japan's mountain passes, with a strong *focus on the driving technique of drifting*. It is widely credited with introducing the drifting motorsport to a global audience.The story is set in the late **1990s** and follows **Takumi Fujiwara**, a quiet and seemingly uninterested high school student. For five years, Takumi has been delivering tofu early every morning to a hotel on *Mount Akina* for his father's shop. During these monotonous runs, he has unknowingly honed his driving skills, mastering the difficult mountain roads in his father's aging *Toyota Sprinter Trueno AE86*.
